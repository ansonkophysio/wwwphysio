Your first visit

A first visit takes about 45–60 minutes.

  1. ConsultationYour history, symptoms and goals
  2. AssessmentJoint movement, strength and everyday movements
  3. ExplanationYour findings and treatment plan, in plain terms
  4. TreatmentTreatment as needed, with exercises for home

Common questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

Before booking, you can ask on WhatsApp whether your situation needs a doctor's referral letter. If you already have a referral letter, medical certificate or reports, please bring them to your first visit.

What should I bring to my first visit?
  • Doctor's referral letter, medical certificate or related reports (if any)
  • X-ray or MRI reports (if any)
  • A list of your current medicines
  • Comfortable clothing
How much does it cost?

HK$900 per physiotherapy session (45–60 minutes). For other treatment fees, please ask on WhatsApp.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and goals. After the first assessment, I'll explain the likely time frame and number of sessions, and what you can practise at home.

Is physiotherapy right for my condition?

If, after the assessment, I think you need to see a doctor or have further tests first, I'll tell you directly and help you find the right professional to follow up.

Can I enquire on behalf of a parent or family member?

Yes. Send a WhatsApp message with a brief description of their condition (for example, where the pain is, or whether they have recently had surgery) and the times that suit them. If they have limited mobility or use a wheelchair, please let me know in advance.
