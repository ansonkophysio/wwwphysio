Your first visit

A first visit takes about 45–60 minutes.

What happens

  1. Your history and symptoms
  2. Examination and movement assessment
  3. The findings and treatment plan, explained
  4. Treatment as needed, plus exercises for home

What to bring

  • Doctor's referral letter, medical certificate or related reports (if any)
  • X-ray or MRI reports (if any)
  • A list of the medicines you take
  • Comfortable clothes you can move in

Fees

HK$900 per physiotherapy session (45–60 minutes). For other treatment fees, please ask on WhatsApp.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

Before your first appointment, please ask on WhatsApp whether your situation needs a doctor's referral letter. If you already have a referral letter, medical certificate or reports, please bring them to your first visit.
