Services

Physiotherapy starts with an assessment, then uses exercise therapy, manual therapy and other methods to help improve pain and movement.

Neck, back and joint pain

Such as neck and back pain from long hours of sitting, wrist and elbow pain, and knee osteoarthritis. Treatment may include exercise therapy, manual therapy and a home exercise programme.

Sports injuries

Such as sprains, strains, knee pain after running or hiking, and recurring old injuries. Treatment is planned around your sport, with a gradual return to training.

Rehab after surgery

Such as rehabilitation after a knee or hip replacement, or ligament surgery, progressed in stages according to your surgeon's instructions and your recovery.

Results vary from person to person. After the assessment, I'll explain the treatment plan, the likely time frame and its limits; if you need to see a doctor first, I'll tell you directly. If symptoms persist or warning signs appear, please see a doctor or physiotherapist.
