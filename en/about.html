About me

Registered Physiotherapist in Hong Kong. Former Hong Kong Water Polo Team player, now the team's physiotherapist.

Hi, I'm Anson Ko

I'm a Registered Physiotherapist in Hong Kong, seeing patients at Amazing Medical in Tsim Sha Tsui.

From 2017 to 2024 I played for the Hong Kong Water Polo Team, representing Hong Kong at the 19th Asian Games and the Asian Water Polo Championship. Seven years of training and competing showed me what athletes deal with every day: the training volume, the pressure of competition, and the urge to get back in the water after an injury.

Today I'm the Team Physiotherapist for the Hong Kong Water Polo Team, and at the 2025 National Games of China I led the team's on-site injury management and recovery. I have also spent two consecutive seasons as field-side physiotherapist for Hong Kong Football Club in the Hong Kong Premier League. As a Diocesan Boys' School (DBS) alumnus, I also provide physiotherapy support to the school's sports teams.

At the clinic, I help people with pain and sports injuries: office workers who sit for long hours, weekend athletes, swimmers and water-sports players, and people recovering after surgery.
