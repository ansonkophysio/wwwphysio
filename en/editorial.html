How these articles are written

Who writes the articles on this site, what they are based on, and their limits.

Author

All articles on this site are written by Anson Ko. I am a Registered Physiotherapist in Hong Kong; you can check my registration on the Physiotherapists Board's list of registrants.

Sources

Articles are based on published research and clinical guidelines. Each article ends with its references, linked to PubMed or the publisher, so you can read them yourself. Where the evidence is not settled, the article says so.

Dates and updates

Each article shows the date it was published, and the date it was updated if the content has been revised. When important new research or guidelines appear, I review and update the articles they affect.

Independence

This site carries no advertising and accepts no sponsorship. The articles do not recommend any product or brand.

Limits

The articles are general health education and do not replace professional diagnosis or treatment. Each article lists the situations in which you should see a doctor promptly; if any apply, or your symptoms persist, please see a doctor or physiotherapist.

Language

The Chinese articles are written in standard written Chinese, using the words people in Hong Kong commonly use, such as 頸梗膊痛 and 拗柴. The articles are also available in English.
