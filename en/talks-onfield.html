Talks & on-field support

Health talks, workshops and on-field physiotherapy support for companies, schools, sports teams and community groups.

Talks and workshops

I give health talks and workshops for companies, schools, sports teams and community groups. They focus on health education, and every session includes demonstrations and practice, so people leave with one or two moves they can use in daily life.

Companies and workplacesSchoolsSports teams and clubsCommunity groups and care homes

Common topics

  • Pain and everyday exercise
  • Neck, shoulder and back pain at the office
  • Injury prevention for running, hiking and ball sports
  • Competition preparation: sleep, warm-up and recovery
  • Strength and fall prevention for older adults

Recent talks

  • October 2026Pain and exercise workshop for older adults (community organisation; one hour, with demonstrations and practice)
  • October 2026Race-week preparation talk (secondary school swim team)

On-field support

I provide field-side physiotherapy for sports teams and events, managing injuries during training and competition and working with coaches and the team's medical staff.

Sports teamsSchool teamsCompetitions and sports events

What's included

  • Field-side injury assessment and immediate care
  • Taping and pre-competition preparation
  • Post-match recovery
  • Deciding whether an athlete can continue, and planning the return to training

Experience

  • Team Physiotherapist, Hong Kong Water Polo Team (current)
  • 2025 National Games of China: on-site injury management and recovery for the Hong Kong water polo team
  • Field-side physiotherapist, Hong Kong Football Club (Hong Kong Premier League), two consecutive seasons
  • Physiotherapy support for Diocesan Boys' School sports teams
